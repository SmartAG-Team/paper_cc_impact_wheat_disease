Hourly meteorological exposures were obtained from the ERA5 reanalysis through the [Open-Meteo Historical Weather API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-weather-api), with the model explicitly fixed to `era5`. The requested fields were 2 m air temperature, 2 m relative humidity, 2 m dew-point temperature, total precipitation, liquid precipitation, 10 m wind speed and surface shortwave radiation. The provider distributes ERA5 on a 0.25° grid. The nearest grid cell was requested, elevation downscaling was disabled, and both requested coordinates and returned grid-cell coordinates were retained. [Weather data by Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), contain modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information.

Each coordinate-year was assigned an inclusive coverage interval from 1 September in the preceding calendar year to 30 September in the observation year. This interval provides autumn, winter, spring and summer weather coverage; it does not represent observed sowing or harvest dates. The BASF source coordinates are rounded to 0.1°, and several trials share a coordinate-year. The Swiss location represents Lindau-Eschikon. The Tunisian coordinates identify the Kodia experimental station. The French coordinates represent the laboratory/greenhouse location at the Thiverval-Grignon station; exact experimental plot coordinates are unavailable. The coordinate provenance and qualification are retained in the external-site linkage table.

Daily variables were calculated from 24 hourly records assigned to the UTC date of their returned timestamps. Temperature summaries comprise hourly mean, minimum and maximum; humidity, dew-point temperature and wind speed are hourly means. Rain and total precipitation are summed. Atmospheric humidity exposure is the number of hours with 2 m relative humidity ≥90%; rain exposure is the number of hours with liquid precipitation >0.1 mm. Daily shortwave energy is the sum of hourly radiation multiplied by 3600/10⁶, expressed in MJ m⁻². Monthly and coverage-interval means use daily means; accumulated quantities use daily sums. Precipitation and rain are preceding-hour accumulations, and radiation is the preceding-hour mean, so the midnight record describes an interval ending at midnight.

Raw response bodies, full requests, timestamps, units, coordinate metadata and SHA-256 checksums are archived. Validation requires the exact hourly timestamp sequence, complete numeric values, expected units, plausible physical ranges and 24 records per UTC day. Hourly and daily outputs have unique location–time and location–date keys. Overlapping September records from consecutive coverage intervals are retained once after confirming numerical agreement between requests. Seasonal summaries retain separate location–year keys.

Coverage-interval summaries include weather after some disease observations and are suitable for describing the archive. Disease-response models require exposure windows ending on or before each observation date. Different trials at the same coordinate-year share the same weather, and different requested coordinates may map to one ERA5 grid cell. Reanalysis cannot resolve field microclimate, canopy humidity or measured leaf wetness; high-humidity hours describe an atmospheric exposure proxy. The short BASF observation period of 2017–2019 supports weather association analyses, while long-term climate-change effects require longer climate records and an explicit temporal or scenario design.
