# European wheat disease and yield study implementation plan

The authorized study chain is field epidemic evaluation, whole-European simulation, and climate-induced disease and crop-yield response. The existing publication and all archived evidence remain preserved. Runtime code remains in model/; fitting, source preparation and observation mapping remain outside model/.

1. Verify source disease identities, ordinal leaf conventions, actual stages, units, observation windows and independent location-year groups. Preserve unresolved definitions and source exceptions.
2. Retrieve genuine public yield contrasts, retained disease observations, treated residual disease, measurement scopes and source trial memberships. Do not use fitted curves as independent observations or missing pathogen measurements as zeros.
3. Evaluate a separate crop-yield response on public Nordic data, with origin-constrained estimators and complete-trial holdouts. Preserve signed observations and shared control dependence. Score the actual identifiable management-response target; absolute disease-free yield loss requires further evidence.
4. Build a resumable full-domain replay wrapper using the frozen current simulator and archived four-field forcing. The wrapper consumes the 14,941-cell area registry, winter/rainfed calendars, historical-only monthly alignment and immutable model configuration. It produces cell-season outcomes, input hashes, coverage and execution receipts. It changes no biological parameters.
5. Verify a current sampled 64-draw year against its corresponding full-domain outputs at the same cells, including all numerical/date metrics. Verify chunk/batch invariance on measured weather. Confirm missing calendars and crop endpoints remain missing rather than zero.
6. Complete the full-domain historical reference and paired future SSP/GCM comparisons. Retain missingness, fixed reference land-use scope and source/model identities. Construct area-weighted ratio estimates from complete cell-season records.
7. Restore geographical research figures. Current-map values come from current-model outputs. Earlier-model ERA5 and climate maps retain separate archival labels. No interpolation creates uncomputed field values.
8. Evaluate complete observed STB epidemic trajectories and independently selected benchmarks; selecting fits or observation mapping uses training membership only. Added diseases require their own observational and model support.
9. Couple only compatible crop-response domains. Retain distinctions between top-three HAD, whole-plant severity, top-four ratings, and fungicide-associated yield response. Quantify supported conditional transfers and their limits rather than inventing a common conversion.
10. Prepare a results-led Nature Food Article from completed analyses: no software architecture or test enumeration in the main narrative, spatial upscaling and climate/yield results in main figures, full validation and software/measurement evidence in supplement. Journal formatting is checked against the publisher's Article requirements.
11. Validate source arithmetic, memberships, common-period pairing, source/model hashes, map presence and native Word/PDF figure embeds before publication files are promoted.
