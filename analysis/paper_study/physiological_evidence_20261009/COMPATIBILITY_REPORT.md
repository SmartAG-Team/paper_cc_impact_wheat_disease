**Priority 3 physiological evidence: acquisition and compatibility assessment, 9 October 2026**

**No new public raw dataset met the joint requirements for physiological canopy–disease–harvest validation. Physiological-model fitting is therefore not justified by this acquisition.** Sixteen source entries were assessed; newly acquired eligible observations and independent environments both equal zero. The fixed HAD-to-yield conversion remains supplementary sensitivity evidence under the Priority 3 completion criterion.

Eligibility requires observed canopy trajectories and leaf-specific disease linked to the same harvested plots, dated crop stages, observed protected/reference comparisons, weather and management, and independent environmental replication. Green leaf area can support a canopy-loss comparison, but does not by itself establish photosynthetic function. Separate calibration of protected crop growth and incremental disease-associated loss requires both outcomes to be observed.

The previously collected Tunisian, Swiss and French plot data remain useful for their original purposes but lack the necessary physiological trajectories and/or reference comparisons. Existing archives were read in place. The 13 Bancal aggregates, eight Foulkes predicted means, 201 Taylor fitted values and 948 additional ordinal disease–yield records were not counted as new physiological observations or downloaded again.

**Concrete unresolved leads.** Bancal's paired tolerance database and the Foulkes/Collin UK experiments have the closest published canopy–harvest designs. Their original dated plot records are the missing asset. The UK 120-site-year archive has unusually relevant GS31 coverage, but a harvest linkage must first be demonstrated. PHENOTOL supplies actionable trial identifiers: 145482 (Ouzouer-le-Marché, 2019), 149157 (Aubigny-aux-Kaisnes, 2021), 146672 (Villers-Saint-Christophe, 2020), and BH-Pheno-Or (Orsonville, 2021). A qualifying public release would need plot/block identifiers, dated manual leaf area, disease ratings, stage dates, harvest and moisture, treatment operations and daily weather. No author contact or data request was sent.

**Units and independence.** Bancal's thermal HAD has units m² leaf m⁻² ground °C day, whereas Foulkes and the UK tolerance study use canopy-area calendar days. Their coefficients are not interchangeable. Percentage greenness, lesion severity and image pixels are not LAI. Grain units include t/ha, q/ha or dt/ha (100 kg/ha), and g/m² (10 kg/ha); dry-matter conversion additionally requires measured or documented moisture. Foulkes and Swiss records specify 15% moisture; unknown moisture is retained as unknown. A leaf or observation date is nested within a plot, and a genotype–management combination is not an independent environment. Protected plots require actual disease observations before being called healthy.

GS31–85 and GS65–85 comparisons require supported stage boundaries and canopy observations across those windows. Heading-based integrals cannot establish GS31 coverage; GS59 and GS83 cannot silently replace GS65 and GS85. Reserve or light-interception extensions require their own measurements and identifiable parameters. Crop-growth configurations also require model-specific soil layers, initial water/nitrogen and forcing beyond the generic intake tables.

**Search and retrieval limits.** The bounded search covered the named leads, relevant author repositories, HAL, DataCite, Recherche Data Gouv, institutional records and official project documentation. Fifteen API queries and 28 selected web queries are logged. The broad Bancal DataCite search returned only the first 100 of 501 records; refined relevant searches are separately recorded with returned/total counts. Indexed publisher content is distinguished from successfully downloaded files. Access failures and unresolved supplements are not evidence that data do not exist. No claim of exhaustive public-data absence follows from these results. Disallowed publishers were excluded from evidence; an incidental repository result was stopped when its linked publisher became apparent.
