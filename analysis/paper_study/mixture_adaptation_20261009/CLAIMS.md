# Empirical management claim

In a randomized durum-wheat experiment at Mauguio in 2018, 195 mixtures with complete harvests and constituent pure-stand references yielded, on average, 0.378 t ha⁻¹ more than their constituent baseline, although 40.5% yielded less. The mean difference was 0.176 t ha⁻¹ when the same comparisons used the source's spatially adjusted estimates. This single-environment comparison describes total harvest performance under the experimental management; it does not identify STB-mediated yield protection or validate adaptation to future climate.

The Swiss release combined mixture trials with external national variety-network controls. Complete constituent references were unavailable within the identified mixture experiment, and matched-source yield differences depended on the external trial group. These diagnostic comparisons do not establish a causal mixture effect.
