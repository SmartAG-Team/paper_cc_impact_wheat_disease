# Empirical cultivar-mixture management comparisons

Protocol version 1, 9 October 2026. Scope: empirical harvest evidence for the research plan's Priority 5; independent of the climate and physiological loss models. Design, identifiers and missingness were inspected before this protocol. Source abstracts and the existing audit already expose published outcomes; this is a prospective analysis specification for this module, not preregistration or outcome-blind research. No module outcome contrasts or fitted models preceded the protocol freeze.

## Populations and comparability

The French source is a single 2017–2018 Mauguio durum-wheat experiment. Pure stands and binary mixtures shared random spatial allocation. Genotype combinations were unreplicated; mixture constituents occupied alternating equal-area rows. Pair construction excluded heading differences exceeding three weeks. Fertilization and post-assessment fungicide protection define the management regime. These experimental inbred lines and the alternating-row arrangement do not establish commercial seed-mixture feasibility. Harvest eligibility uses the full author-retained phenotype population, independent of whether STB was scored. Two author-retained mixtures have only three raw harvest-row values; a four-row completeness rule excludes them from the raw primary endpoint. Pure-stand pooled harvest samples duplicated into row representations are not independent replicates.

The Swiss release combines randomized mixture experiments with national variety-network pure stands. Identified mixture-trial pure checks are HANSWIN and MONTALBANO; no mixture has all its constituents among those checks. A shared postcode and harvest year is insufficient evidence of exchangeability, equal management or co-randomization. Pure stands are grouped by source `ww` trial code, never pooled across codes. The meanings and management regimes of external trial codes remain unresolved. Mixtures lacking physical plot identifiers include reference/derived rows and are ineligible as observed mixture harvests. Current release values control observation inclusion; older release fields recover identifiers, block, trial code and additional constituents through exact shared-field matches, never add an independent season or replicate.

## Outcomes and units

The experimental unit is one physical plot-season, identified in France by row and column within Mauguio 2018, and in Switzerland by recovered plot identifier. A comparison is one mixture plot and its constituent pure-stand references. Source CSV line numbers, input hashes and all control IDs remain traceable.

French GY is g m⁻²; multiplication by 0.01 yields t ha⁻¹ on the source dried-grain basis (moisture percentage unspecified). Plot yield is the arithmetic mean of four equal-area row representations, requiring four finite values. The file named RAW_GY contains author spatial BLUPs, not raw harvest measurements; these are a separately labeled sensitivity on the same eligible comparisons. Swiss yield_dtha is dt ha⁻¹, multiplied by 0.1; the prior source archive records a 15% grain-moisture basis, which the current README does not independently specify. No cross-source moisture adjustment or pooled effect is supported. Swiss protein is percent dry matter and specific weight is kg hl⁻¹. French thousand-kernel weight (TKW) is g and remains a measured yield component, not a milling-quality or price measure. Ordinal STB scores retain source scale; they are not percent leaf area or functional canopy loss.

## Fixed comparisons and aggregation

Primary French endpoint: mixture plot yield minus the arithmetic mean of its two observed constituent pure stands in the same experiment. Relative difference is 100 times this difference divided by that baseline. Both controls and all four harvest rows must be present. Every eligible mixture has equal weight within the one trial. Plot-total overyielding and component relative yield total (RYT = mean of component mixture/pure ratios) are different estimands and receive different column names. The source RYT based on BLUPs is never substituted for an observed plot-total yield ratio.

Swiss endpoint: diagnostic difference against the equal-constituent mean of pure-stand means within the identical year, postcode and external `ww` code. Control replication is averaged within cultivar first. Both constituents must have finite harvest values in the same control group. Binary mixtures are eligible; four-way mixtures lack a documented composition weight and are excluded from equal-mass constituent comparisons. Exact duplicate control records without distinct physical identifiers are collapsed conservatively, with source aliases retained. A unique old-release match is required to recover `ww`; ambiguous records are quarantined, not guessed. The 50:50 seed-mass rule supports an operational arithmetic reference, not equal plant numbers.

Swiss comparisons against same-block HANSWIN/MONTALBANO checks, if observed, are a separate feasibility diagnostic. Check membership is supported by plot/block identifiers, but explicit co-randomization of pure checks is not confirmed by the dataset README. These comparisons do not identify a mixture-diversity effect and receive no validated causal flag.

Within each Swiss environment and control group, mixture replicates are averaged by composition first and compositions equally second. Environments receive equal weight within each control-code analysis. Different source control codes remain separate analyses; no outcome-based choice of the favorable code occurs. France and Switzerland remain separate evidence domains and are never combined into a single adaptation estimate.

## Uncertainty, dependence and transfer

Swiss uncertainty is a seeded percentile bootstrap of sites (10,000 draws, seed 20261009), retaining both years, all compositions, replicates and shared controls together. Within each draw environment means retain equal trial weight; site repeats are cluster multiplicities. This interval measures stability of noncausal matched-source differences with only five possible sites. It does not repair nonexchangeability or represent a calibrated European confidence interval. Report site and environment counts, effective coverage and observed environment ranges.

French comparison dependency groups are connected components of the constituent-sharing graph. There are four groups, with 189 of 195 complete raw comparisons in the largest group. No independent-comparison confidence interval or cross-environment interval is estimable from this structure. Report missing intervals and explicit reasons. Leave-one-genotype-out summaries remove every comparison involving that genotype and quantify composition sensitivity, not independent validation or confidence bounds. Spatial-grid row/column deletion removes every comparison whose mixture or either control occupies the removed coordinate; those influence ranges also are not confidence bounds. Connected-group summaries remain available for auditing shared controls.

Swiss leave-one-site-out and forward-year predictions use only the equal-environment training mean of diagnostic differences, against a zero-difference reference. No tuning or alternative models are selected. Predictions apply to held-out environment means with equal trial weight. Cultivars may recur; site holdouts do not demonstrate cultivar transfer, and repeated sites across years do not demonstrate geographic transfer. The French single site-year has no external environmental holdout. Author BLUPs use the full trial and are descriptive sensitivity, never held-out predictions.

## Downside, selection and measured trade-offs

Report the fraction of observed comparisons below their constituent baseline, empirical lower decile, minimum, and mean. Swiss downside is computed after replicate aggregation with equal composition and environment weights. These are observed-sample distributions, not probabilities of future failure or annual production stability. Yield versus the higher observed constituent is explicitly a hindsight selection-on-observed-yields diagnostic; it is neither an independently selected cultivar benchmark nor a deployable policy. No best mixture is selected using its evaluation outcome.

Swiss protein and specific-weight differences use complete matched controls for that trait; denominators are reported separately. French TKW aggregates harvest samples by estimated kernel number: total grain mass divided by sum of grain mass/TKW, with the same equal-area convention for constituent baselines. It receives only measured yield-component interpretation. No economic return, protein, pesticide saving, drought tolerance or market acceptance is imputed where not observed. Disease is retained as early/ordinal context only; no yield decomposition or mediation is fitted.

## Applicability and stopping rules

Machine-readable records distinguish (1) descriptive association, (2) randomized within-trial treatment-composition comparisons conditional on complete harvests, (3) hindsight selection, (4) climate adaptation and (5) disease-mediated grain loss. France supports restricted within-trial harvest comparisons, with no independent combination replication or environmental transfer. Swiss external comparisons are noncausal. Neither source validates climate adaptation, STB-mediated grain loss, European production benefits, commercial adoption, or the climate model. Failed coverage or design gates remain explicit numerical audit outputs; they cannot be replaced by a positive adaptation claim.

## Reproduction and verification contract

Only files within this module are writable. The compact public-input bundle includes original source bytes, source code needed to interpret BLUPs, dataset attribution, hashes and provenance. The run verifies protocol and input hashes before analysis. Tests cover unit conversion, physical-plot aggregation, constituent completeness, duplicate and ambiguous matches, no cross-trial pooling, control reuse, equal trial weighting, grouped resampling, holdout leakage, measured-trait denominators, and unsupported-claim flags. Deterministic reruns must reproduce numerical tables byte for byte. The parent builder, previous modules, root documentation, branch and index remain untouched.
