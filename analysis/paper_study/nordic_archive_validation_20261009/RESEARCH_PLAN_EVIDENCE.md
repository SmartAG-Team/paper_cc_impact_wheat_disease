### Nordic/Baltic archive: observed management–grain evidence

Harmonization of the Nordic/Baltic 2012–2016 archive yields 307 treated-minus-untreated grain contrasts from 263 reported trial identifiers in five countries and 25 country–year groups. Observed yields are expressed at 15% grain moisture. Ambiguous treatment keys, absent controls and missing yields are excluded through source-row audits; 44 shared untreated summaries support two management contrasts each. The trial-weighted mean gain is 0.980 t ha⁻¹ (95% country–year cluster-bootstrap interval 0.804–1.142). Negative responses remain in the evidence. [Source datasets](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.19203377.v4), [SAS documentation and derived tables](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.19203398.v3).

A fixed pre-fit protocol supports country–year, country, year and retrospective forward-year holdouts. Primary held-out RMSE is 0.628 t ha⁻¹ for the training mean, 0.636 for a treatment-category benchmark and 0.638 for a crop–treatment benchmark. The management benchmarks provide no improvement on the primary endpoint. An inconsistent source-reported gain is replaced by the direct difference between its observed yields, with an exclusion sensitivity that preserves this conclusion. These comparisons establish a management-associated grain baseline; they do not identify causal treatment effects.

The archive contains no observed severity–yield pairs, disease-species observations, leaf identity, repeated disease assessments, measured LAI or verified healthy-yield reference. Weather-derived treatment recommendations remain distinct from disease measurements. Regional average stage dates have no trial-year measurement status, and their date/day-number discrepancy remains explicit. Endpoint-versus-leaf/stage-aware disease comparisons and physiological yield-loss validation remain unavailable. The archive therefore advances the trial–treatment measurement inventory and independent grouping component of Priority 1, while the cause-specific and physiological requirements of Priority 3 remain unmet.

The evidence package includes exact CC BY 4.0 public-source inputs, row-level provenance, explicit denominator definitions, environmental memberships, training-only benchmarks and standalone numerical reproduction. Reported trial identifiers do not establish independent plot replication; recurrent equal control values remain audited within their country–year groups. The results are existing-field management evidence and do not validate future climate responses or European production impacts.
