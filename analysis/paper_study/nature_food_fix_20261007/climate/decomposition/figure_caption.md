Weather–host decomposition of modeled SSP585 crop-infection timing and canopy damage for 2071–2100 relative to 1991–2020. Left, flag-leaf symptom timing relative to anthesis. Right, upper-three-leaf GS65–85 HAD loss proxy. Disease-weather and host-development Shapley contributions average the two possible factor orders and sum to the total change. The interaction is reported separately and is divided equally between these contributions. Gray segments denote the three-GCM ranges, colored points denote GCM means, and colored bars denote ±1 spatial Monte Carlo standard error with shared spatial-draw covariance retained. Each metric uses the historical–future pairs with finite outputs in all four weather–host combinations. Existing daily climate forcing is aligned by elapsed days from sowing; each retained host trajectory preserves its own stage dates, GS85 endpoint and integration window. The contributions are within-model quantities without physical causal or actual-yield attribution.
