The state contains susceptible tissue and two route-specific blocks of latent stages, nonsporulating visible damage N, infectious tissue I and noninfectious damaged tissue R. Damage is N+I+R; pycnidial coverage is I+R. R ceases to contribute infectious pressure but remains in visible damage and pycnidial-structure coverage until cohort renewal or reset. These outputs are normalized cohort fractions rather than spore counts or whole-canopy measurements. The primary label represents effective external infection pressure. Secondary flow depends on modeled infectious fractions and rain; the route of individual observed lesions is not identified by field severity records.

Latent and nonsporulating progression use thermal rate max(T,0)/18. Thus the latent_days and nonsporulating_days parameters are reference-temperature durations at 18°C, with base-zero linear thermal scaling. Infectious removal uses calendar time. The establishment Gaussian remains positive at nonpositive temperatures while progression stops, allowing latent infection to accumulate under the assumed forcing response. This is a mathematical property of the specified response, not an independently validated low-temperature infection constraint. Two input-contract errors require correction before broader source use. First, `prepare_fields` does not check the source unit or restrict eligible metrics. A synthetic ordinal-leaf lesion count with values 1 and 2 is accepted and assigned the percent damage proxy. Second, the leaf regular expression matches a prefix without terminating the rank token: both F10 and LEAF, 14 become leaf index zero. The current BASF archive contains the intended percent metric and valid ordinal ranks, so these errors do not alter its frozen result. General-source eligibility requires percent-compatible metric/unit checks and complete ordinal-rank token matching. Disease observations with incompatible units must retain an exclusion reason.

The implemented weights equalize coordinate-years, then share each coordinate-year's weight among source fields, observation series and assessments. Calibration has 26 coordinate-years and 28 fields; validation has 36 coordinate-years and 45 fields. Field total weights range from 0.5 to 1 in calibration and from 0.25 to 1 in validation. The reported aggregate metrics therefore differ from equal-field-season metrics. The protocol's present field-season-weighted terminology does not describe the implemented estimator. Either the declared estimand must identify equal coordinate-year weighting or a separately prespecified equal-field-season score is required. Metric choice cannot be selected for favorable validation performance.

Host emergence uses previous-day phenological accumulation. The final seven cohort emergence thresholds interpolate between the source BBCH31 and BBCH51 events; early thresholds can coincide at emergence because of clipping. These are explicit structural approximations. Only the juvenile reservoir undergoes thermal-time turnover. Final cohorts do not undergo within-leaf area expansion or senescence and remain active after emergence; BBCH85 is checked for threshold ordering but does not deactivate host.
