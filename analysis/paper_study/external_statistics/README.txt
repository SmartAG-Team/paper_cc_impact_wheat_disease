Frozen seasonal-model external statistics

The analysis uses the unchanged Corteva external archive and the BASF calibration-only parameter and leaf-rank-baseline archives. It imports no model module, performs no parameter fitting and selects no hyperparameters. All prediction, parameter and archive hashes are recorded in receipt.json and independently checked in independent_validation.json.

There are 1,721 observed ordinal-leaf assessments from 145 source field-seasons in 106 coordinate-year clusters. The strict location-disjoint scope contains 143 fields,1,703 assessments and 104 clusters after excluding two rounded-coordinate sites that also occur in the complete raw BASF source. Rounded coordinate identity is a clustering convention and does not prove physical field identity. Every evaluated field has positive observed-window disease. Final severity is the last numeric assessment for each source field/leaf series:630 series in the full scope and 621 in the strict scope. It is not an independently observed season-end canopy severity.

Paired 95% percentile confidence intervals use 10,000 coordinate-year resamples, seed 20261006. Whole coordinate-year clusters are sampled with replacement. Model and baseline predictions use identical resamples; all leaf subsets, endpoint choices, weighting variants, sensitivities and onset censoring strata within a scope reuse the full-cohort resample indices. The intervals condition on the frozen fitted parameters and observed external fields; fitting uncertainty is excluded. Dependence between years at the same source coordinate is not explicitly resampled.

The principal weighting is literal_equal_coordinate_year: equal selected coordinate-year clusters; equal fields within each cluster; equal leaf series within each field; equal assessments within each series. The final-assessment endpoint contains one assessment per series. literal_equal_field is supplementary: equal fields, equal series within a field and equal assessments within a series, while the bootstrap still resamples complete coordinate-year clusters. archived_selected_weights reproduces the original archived weights without alteration. Original upper-three all-assessment weights were inherited from the full leaf set without renormalization after the leaf restriction; their coordinate-year totals vary. These archived scores and the literal weighting variants remain separately labelled. Final-assessment archived and literal equal-coordinate-year weights coincide.

severity_point_estimates.csv contains 96 point estimates. severity_bootstrap_intervals.csv contains 432 model, baseline and paired-difference 95% intervals. Errors and differences are in percentage points; bias is predicted minus observed. Negative paired RMSE or MAE differences favour the seasonal model. data/paper_study/external_statistics/severity_bootstrap_draws.parquet retains 1,440,000 metric draws, severity_cluster_scores.csv retains sufficient cluster statistics, and coordinate_year_draw_indices.npz plus coordinate_year_membership.csv retain exact resampling indices.

Onset is first observed threshold crossing at 0.1%,1% or 5%, never an observed infection event. Left-censoring has a positive first visible assessment and no earlier observed below-threshold assessment; interval-censoring uses the last observed below-threshold date before the first positive date; right-censoring has no observed threshold crossing. Predicted visibility is compatible when it lies in the observed interval, before/on the left-censor upper bound, or after the right-censor lower bound. Daily intervals use the first admissible day after the last negative assessment; a prediction on the last negative day has signed bound distance−1 day. Negative distance means premature visibility, positive distance means delayed visibility and zero means compatibility. Exact onset errors and infection-date errors cannot be identified. Missing predicted dates remain explicit. Stratum estimates give equal weight to represented coordinate-year clusters and equal fields within clusters, with equal leaf series within fields. The common full-cohort draws can omit a rare stratum entirely; these draws remain missing and finite-bootstrap counts are reported rather than replaced with zero.

onset_censoring_statistics.csv reports compatibility, intervals, censoring counts and signed/absolute distances. data/paper_study/external_statistics/onset_reconstructed_bounds.csv and onset_bootstrap_draws.parquet retain bounds and resamples. Left-censor compatibility is a permissive one-sided check and cannot establish accurate onset. Small right-censored strata do not represent full-season disease absence. A bootstrap stratum with no compatible observations can have a degenerate zero interval; this does not establish zero compatibility in the underlying population.

observed_window_classification.csv reports field-level threshold detection at evaluated assessment dates. At 0.1%, all 145 full-scope and 143 strict-scope fields are positive. Specificity is undefined and an always-positive warning gives the same classifications as both disease models and the baseline. At 1% and 5%, one or two below-threshold field windows are present; they do not establish full-season healthy controls or reliable specificity. Predicted severity fractions are not calibrated occurrence probabilities.

The source-date sensitivity removes only the original 2015-58 assessment dated 2015-10-30 from scoring. Earlier frozen predictions and predicted onset dates remain unchanged; final leaf endpoints and observed censoring bounds are reconstructed from remaining observations. Its source date is retained in all original archives. No date is corrected, no season is refitted, and the anomaly flag preceded external scoring.

In the strict scope, final-severity equal-coordinate-year RMSE is 35.93 percentage points versus 46.61 for the frozen leaf-rank baseline: paired difference−10.68,95% interval[−13.05,−8.14]. MAE is 29.00 versus 36.32: paired difference−7.32,[−9.76,−4.76]. Bias is−17.44 percentage points. The final upper-three RMSE difference is−15.37,[−18.03,−12.53]. Literal equal-field final RMSE is 37.60 versus 47.90. Removing the flagged late assessment gives strict final RMSE 35.57 versus 46.61, paired difference−11.04,[−13.26,−8.69]. Absolute severity errors and underprediction remain substantial despite better errors than this frozen baseline.

At 0.1% in the strict scope, equal-coordinate-year onset compatibility is 18.8%,95% interval[12.2%,26.0%], among 145 interval-censored leaf series. The 117 incompatible finite predictions have median signed bound distance−12 days. Left-censored compatibility is 95.6%,[92.3%,98.2%], among 465 series; right-censored compatibility is 0 among 11 series in 5 coordinate-year clusters. These results do not establish accurate infection timing or disease occurrence discrimination.

The observed source metric is infection P%INF/INFECT with an unspecified denominator, represented by a working disease-severity observation operator. Observed sowing dates, cultivar identities and infection-event dates are unavailable. No-foliar-spray status does not resolve seed treatment, inoculation or all management conditions. Simulations use historical realized ERA 5 weather and calendar priors; these hindcast statistics do not include the future-weather uncertainty of an operational season-start forecast. The effective external hazard and the near-zero fitted secondary coefficient do not establish separately identified inoculum sources or absence of secondary transmission. External source and measurement comparability remain limited.

Python dependencies are NumPy, pandas and PyArrow. Tested versions are recorded in requirements.txt. The independent verification reconstructs all point metrics and bootstrap draws, original archived severity and onset scores, leaf-rank-baseline predictions, observed censor bounds, occurrence counts, draw generation, strict source-coordinate disjointness, numerical bounds and archive/fit hashes. It passed 16,563 checks with zero failures; maximum bootstrap discrepancy is 3.55e-14 percentage points and maximum archived severity-metric discrepancy is 1.78e-14 percentage points.
