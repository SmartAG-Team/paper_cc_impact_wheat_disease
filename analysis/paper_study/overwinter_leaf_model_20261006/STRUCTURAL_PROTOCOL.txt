Wheat leaf development and seasonal Septoria dynamics

The crop clock retains the frozen temperature–photoperiod–vernalization process. Additional stem and flag-leaf stages are calibrated from stage observations in the original BASF 2017–2018 field registry, independently of disease assessments. BBCH37 denotes flag-leaf visibility; BBCH39 denotes full unfolding. Stem-node stages are distinct from leaf counts. Final-rank emergence spacing remains an explicit sensitivity because individually tracked emergence and absolute final leaf numbers are absent.

Local carryover comprises infected wheat residue and the living autumn–winter canopy. Relative unready and competent residue states represent an assumed initial local source, weather-dependent development and thermal ageing. Their units are relative source potential, not measured spore abundance. Initial source potential and primary establishment scale are confounded; their product is identifiable only conditionally. No mandatory spring activation stage is imposed. Seedling infection is possible after crop emergence.

Susceptible, latent, visible noninfectious, infectious and retained symptomatic tissue states persist on active leaves. Latent progression is temperature dependent. Expanding tissue dilutes affected fractions, and turnover renews the juvenile canopy. Secondary establishment requires infectious donor tissue and suitable weather. Rain-mediated transfer and contact during unfolding are separate hypotheses. An optional imported pressure represents airborne inoculum from outside the plot. Local and imported source contributions cannot be distinguished from the available disease assessments.

Disease calibration uses zero/positive labels and first-symptom intervals, prioritizing the top three leaves. Infection dates are latent model events; visible symptoms provide the observational constraints. A fixed effective detection threshold and its sensitivity are explicit observation assumptions. Percentage magnitudes do not determine the primary fit.

All fitted stage and disease parameters are frozen before BASF2019 and location-disjoint Corteva evaluation. These archives have contributed to earlier development, so the evaluation is retrospective development validation. Timing metrics retain censoring; interval distance is not exact-date MAE. All candidate settings and source hashes precede fitting. Mechanistic ablations distinguish missing local carryover, removed secondary spread, and imported-source assumptions. Absolute yield loss requires observed healthy leaf-area trajectories; standardized top-three-leaf healthy-area-duration transfer remains conditional on declared leaf-area and tolerance assumptions.
