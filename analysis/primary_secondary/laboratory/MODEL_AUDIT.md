# Epidemic-model mathematical and data audit

The audited implementation and evaluation contract are retained byte-identically in `parent_model_audit_source/`. `parent_model_audit.json` records their SHA256 identities and 40 passing checks. Source files were unchanged during the audit. Numba and Python execution agree exactly across 21 trajectory arrays at latent-stage counts 1, 3 and 5.

Susceptible tissue and the three infection-origin blocks conserve unit tissue mass. Nonnegative exponential flows share susceptible losses between external and secondary hazards in proportion to their competing hazards. Latent, nonsporulating, infectious and removed transitions use the preceding substep state. Damage equals N+I+R, pycnidia equals I+R, and infectious pressure uses I. Newly acquired origin totals increase exactly by their corresponding infection fluxes; unresolved initial-origin totals remain constant. Inactive ranks retain S=1 and supply no transmission or latent tissue. The numerical invariant tests include randomized forcing, high bounded transmission coefficients, zero-source cases, primary-only transmission, rain-free secondary transmission and exact exponential infectious removal.

Initialization uses assessments dated no later than the target episode's first assessment. Perturbing later disease values leaves the initial canopy unchanged. Every scored target follows its conditioning observation. Forward folds remove test coordinates from earlier-year fitting; country and inner folds preserve location separation. Fitting weights match the declared equal-coordinate-year, equal-series, equal-future-observation score. The 91 episodes contain 224 scored future assessments. This chronology supports conditional transfer hindcasts. Earlier baseline inspection prevents interpretation as newly untouched test evidence.

The following limitations affect statistical or process interpretation:

| Finding | Evidence and implication |
| --- | --- |
| Bootstrap units omit repeated-location dependence | France has 8 coordinate-years from 6 locations, Poland 7 from 5, and Netherlands 2 from 1. Sampling coordinate-years treats repeated years at one location as independent. Location-block resampling must retain all years and leaves while preserving the equal-coordinate-year score. |
| Singleton-location country uncertainty is unavailable | Bulgaria, Czech Republic, Denmark, Ireland, Netherlands and Slovakia each supply one independent test location. A between-location confidence interval is unidentified; repeated draws of one cluster create an artificial zero-width range. |
| The weather ablation retains thermal development | `weather_response=False` disables establishment and splash responses while temperature still controls latent and N→I rates. With initial latent fraction 0.5 over 40 days, 5 versus 25 °C changes damage by 31.12 percentage points. This variant tests transmission-weather dependence, rather than removal of every weather input. |
| Initial infectious composition is assumed | All visible initial tissue enters I, although the damage measurement pools N, I and R. The initial observation does not identify its infectious proportion. Uniform initial latent-stage allocation likewise assumes unresolved infection ages. |
| Earlier canopy scores are carried forward | Forty-three other-rank values in 27 episodes precede the episode start, by up to 41 days. Their visible percentages enter current I without intervening aging, removal or senescence. This is causal use of available information, with an unvalidated current-state approximation. |
| Finite-step progression approximates continuous Erlang timing | At 18 °C and a quarter-day step, a fresh three-stage latent cohort has mean waiting time 20.377 days for the nominal 20-day parameter. The nonsporulating mean is 3.127 days for the nominal 3-day parameter. The combined E→I mean is 23.504 days; the exact gamma interpretation applies in the continuous-time limit. |
| Historical damage and pycnidia remain monotone | Three observed series contain one decline each, of 0.5, 1.0 and 1.967 percentage points. These changes cannot be reproduced by the monotone state mapping and require observation-error or denominator interpretation. |
| Inactive-rank semantics differ from one SPEC sentence | The current SPEC assigns a global latent fraction to unassessed ranks; the implementation excludes these ranks from both latent initialization and transmission. The implementation agrees with the latest inactive-rank instruction. |
| Episode padding lacks observed forcing | Padding after an episode's last target uses 18 °C and zero humidity/rain. Scores access only actual target dates; padded trajectories cannot support later forecast or source-attribution claims. |

The quarter-day versus eighth-day synthetic damage difference is 0.637 percentage points under the audited stress forcing. This is a numerical sensitivity measurement, not a tolerance established from field observations. The fitted field run's convergence result remains a separate requirement.

Alpha and beta have day⁻¹ coefficient units because their establishment, splash and weighted infectious-fraction multipliers are dimensionless. Alpha describes a modeled external infection hazard; it does not measure airborne ascospore supply. Beta describes conditional tissue transmission within the assumed rank kernel, not conidia deposited or dispersed per unit leaf area. Two-metre RH exposure is an atmospheric proxy, and the temperature optimum, response width, wetness scale, splash scale and rank kernel are structural assumptions. Tissue-origin labels provide conditional simulated attribution; the initial origin remains unresolved.

The controlled-inoculation analysis estimates plant/leaf presence-time distributions rather than tissue-area fractions or emitted spores. First detectable pycnidia, combined damage, resistant controls and failed or late establishment require separate observation and host-response assumptions. These data do not identify the simulator's airborne-primary coefficient, a universal temperature response or a successful-infection-conditioned gamma rate. German phenology evaluation provides no Septoria process validation. Numerical correctness and chronological separation therefore coexist with unvalidated process attribution, host transfer and continental application.
