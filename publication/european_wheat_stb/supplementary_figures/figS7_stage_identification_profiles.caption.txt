Figure S7 | Ordered stage-threshold identification from calibration-only observations. Each blue curve is the minimum joint loss conditional on that threshold, after optimizing the other ordered stage thresholds. Dashed vertical lines mark the selected values; horizontal lines show declared one- and two-day loss tolerances. The displayed range is zero to three days above the joint minimum; all 399 grid values for each event remain in Source Data. Loss-tolerance regions describe identification and are not statistical confidence intervals.
