Data availability

Source data and numerical evidence supporting the findings are deposited at https://github.com/SmartAG-Team/paper_cc_impact_wheat_disease, with the manuscript-specific files in publication/european_wheat_stb. The repository is publicly accessible. Dataset references identify the public disease archives, the Nordic Field Trial System, Tunisian cultivar-mixture observations, German multi-environment field data, Swiss and French cultivar-mixture observations, NEX-GDDP-CMIP6v2 climate data, GGCMI calendars, SPAM2020 wheat area and EEA environmental boundaries. Source_Data.xlsx contains completed grid summaries, environmental-region and country statistics, area shares by direction of change, pooled field evaluation, published canopy–yield evidence, grouped yield-response tests, fixed-production exposure and common-population timing comparisons. Original labels and assessment definitions remain in the underlying records. [14, 16, 17, 18, 24, 25, 26, 27, 28, 29, 30]

The repository includes completed summaries, selected diagnostic outputs, input and output hashes, and numerical evidence for document regeneration. The complete raw climate and annual-output archives are external because of their size. Model reruns require the recorded climate products and adjustment files. Raw plot observations and physiological measurements unavailable in the source studies are not reconstructed.

Code availability

Simulation, statistical-analysis and document-generation code are deposited in the same repository, which is publicly accessible. The repository includes evidence manifests and an executed field-yield validation notebook. REPRODUCIBILITY.txt documents regeneration and model-rerun requirements. The supplementary model specification contains 29 numbered equations, and Parameter_inventory.csv records 84 fixed, fitted and scenario quantities.
