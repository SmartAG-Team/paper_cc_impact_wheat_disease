The complete-season support retains 1,902 of 1,920 spatial draw-years per climate model, 62 unique cells and 99.6407% of the original sampled-design area-time weight. The preregistered common-calendar support retains 1,656 draw-years, 57 unique cells and 77.4551% of that weight. Both supports require all five options to complete in both periods and all three climate models; the latter also requires a nonempty all-option shared calendar interval. These results are sampled-design sensitivities.

A fixed 14-day delay changes the future own-window symptom-tissue proxy by -1.6129 days on complete-season support and -2.3300 days on common-calendar support. On the broader support, grain-fill mean temperature increases by 1.2780 degrees Celsius and Tmax-above-30 exposure increases by 2.0450 days.

Complete-season leave-one-climate-model-out selection gives a 14-day advance in Steppic cells in all three folds, where the original common-calendar selection gives a 14-day delay. Atlantic, Boreal, Continental and Mediterranean cells retain the 14-day delay; sparse Pannonian and Unassigned cells retain unchanged sowing. The selected shifts reach the tested bounds and do not establish an interior, agronomic, economic or yield optimum.

The same complete-season-trained regional policy changes the own-window proxy by -1.8912 days on complete-season support and -2.1089 days on common-calendar support. Its shared-time change is -1.8820 days on the latter. On complete-season support, modeled grain-fill duration changes by 1.9822 days, mean temperature by -0.3458 degrees Celsius and Tmax-above-30 exposure by -0.0764 days. Whole-window and shared-time values describe different temporal supports; the shared endpoint is unavailable for complete seasons with zero common-window overlap. Tissue/window responses do not establish physiological or grain-yield benefits.
