Published quantitative evidence was classified by disease attribution, response definition, leaf and time scope, experimental independence and availability of sampling variances. The French annual series was transcribed from the numerical labels in the original ARVALIS chart and checked against the embedded source image. Quintals per hectare were divided by ten. Each published year received equal descriptive weight because annual trial counts were unavailable. The mean, median, range, quartiles and standard deviation describe the 22 annual network summaries. Their variability is not a sampling confidence interval. The overlapping 2004–2017 STB-dominated trial account was not pooled with these annual summaries. No retrieved set supplied compatible independent STB-specific grain-yield effects with adequate sampling variances, so a new inverse-variance meta-analysis and canopy-to-grain conversion were not identifiable from the accessible evidence.
