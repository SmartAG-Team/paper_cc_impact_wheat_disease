Appendix: independent adaptation verification
The adaptation parquet contains 57,600 unique model/reference-year/draw/period/shift rows: 64 spatial draws, 62 unique cells, three GCMs, two 30-year periods and five shifts. Independent reconstruction from actual BBCH65/BBCH85 dates exactly reproduces all-option calendar intersections, their lengths and nonempty flags. Reconstruction over the 30 rows per draw/reference-year exactly reproduces the strict common-three-GCM support mask. Every exported option-minus-zero contrast equals the record-level subtraction exactly. Recomparison of zero-shift whole-window records with retained attribution y00/y11 gives a maximum difference of 1.42108547×10⁻14, consistent with 180 season-level checks.

The strict-panel +14-day result is correctly −2.33004499 own-window days and −2.40095605 shared-calendar days. Regional policy choices reproduced directly from training-member record means are +14 in Atlantic, Boreal, Continental, Mediterranean and Steppic; single-cell Pannonian and Unassigned fall back to zero. Reweighting held-member records reproduces the domain policy effect −2.25039148 days, with +0.74961485°C and +1.45405169 Tmax>30°C days. The complete-season sensitivity described in item 1.1 instead gives a held-policy domain effect of −1.89119012 days after allowing the Steppic choice to change. No raw record or numerical mismatch was detected in the declared strict analysis.

The calendar intersection is an actual-date contrast within the same weather season; it differs from the earlier factorial attribution's alignment of weather and host sequences by elapsed crop age. A shared-calendar reduction excludes one simple explanation based solely on a longer/shorter integration interval, but does not isolate a direct disease response with host state held constant. Sowing changes crop development, tissue states, exposure and the timing of the window simultaneously.

The smallest sampled objective occurs at the declared +14-day bound in most zones, so no interior optimum is established. The bounded calendar checks do not validate agronomic feasibility: establishment moisture, workability, winter survival, rotation, cultivar suitability, yield and economics are absent. The policy uses only training GCM objective values, but selection and evaluation share a fixed spatial sample and a support definition involving all members. It establishes conditional within-ensemble numerical behavior, not field transfer or an implementable continental strategy. The large Steppic support reversal makes that distinction empirically consequential rather than merely formal.
