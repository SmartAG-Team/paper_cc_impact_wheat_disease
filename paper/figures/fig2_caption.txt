Figure 2. Retained field observations and sampling characteristics. (a) All 128 source locations over SPAM2020 physical wheat area, expressed as a percentage of native 0.25° cell area (IFPRI, 2026). Circles denote BASF and triangles denote Corteva. Grey land is outside mapped wheat support; pale blue denotes water. (b) Assessment counts by season and source. (c) Recorded disease-score distributions in 10-percentage-point bins; the final bin includes 100%. (d) Unique assessment dates per field-season. The 2,304 assessments represent 218 field-seasons during 2014–2019 and explicitly untreated, zero-spray controls. Each assessment aggregates one field, date and source-native leaf category. Native score and anatomical definitions remain qualified. Natural Earth outlines provide geographic context (Natural Earth, n.d.). Map lines delineate study areas and do not necessarily depict accepted national boundaries.
