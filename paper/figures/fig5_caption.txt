Figure 5. Climate-response magnitude and direction agreement. (a) SSP1–2.6; (b) SSP2–4.5; (c) SSP5–8.5: equal-three-model mean change in normalized affected-tissue duration, 2071–2100 relative to 1991–2020. (d) Three-model direction agreement under SSP5–8.5. Coloured cells represent wheat within 14,941 native 0.25° footprints, with common EPSG:3035 extent. Grey land is outside mapped wheat support; pale blue denotes water. Dark grey denotes unavailable responses. In (a)–(c), white is zero disease change within wheat cells. The shared colour bar retains changes beyond ±12 days through its triangular ends. Model agreement is not a probability; affected-tissue duration is a symptom-tissue proxy. Natural Earth outlines provide geographic context (Natural Earth, n.d.). Map lines delineate study areas and do not necessarily depict accepted national boundaries.
